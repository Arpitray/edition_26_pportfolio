PORTFOLIO ART - IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
========================================

The idea: classical oil paintings with modern objects in them (laptop, phone, charts), like
the reference site. Each scene is NOT one picture. It is generated as separate layers, so on
scroll the layers can come apart: the figure drifts forward, objects float off, the background
stays behind. If a scene is generated as one flat image, it cannot do that.


1. HOW EVERY SCENE IS BUILT (4 kinds of layer)
----------------------------------------------
  L0  BACKGROUND PLATE   the room or landscape with NOBODY in it, full frame, 16:9.
                         It must be fully painted behind where the figure will stand,
                         because the figure moves and the area behind it shows.
  L1  FIGURE             the person alone, cut out (transparent background), full body
                         or the part that is visible in the scene.
  L2  FOREGROUND         the nearest things (a table edge, drapery, plants), cut out.
                         They frame the scene and move the most.
  L3  OBJECTS            each modern object alone, cut out, one file per object
                         (laptop, phone, mug, chart, badge...). These are what float away.

Same light in every layer of a scene: the light direction is written in each prompt; keep it.


2. STYLE BLOCK (paste at the end of EVERY prompt)
-------------------------------------------------
  Style: original classical oil painting of a quiet 1660s European interior, soft natural
  window light from the upper left, warm ochre, deep ultramarine and muted green palette,
  fine visible brushwork, subtle canvas texture, realistic proportions, highly detailed,
  calm and dignified mood.

  Modern objects are painted in the SAME oil-paint style (not photoreal, not glossy 3D).
  One accent colour only: hot magenta (#FF2E88).

  IMPORTANT: never name a real painter or a real painting in a prompt (no "Vermeer", no
  "The Geographer", no "in the style of ..."). Image tools refuse those as copying third-party
  work ("similarity to third-party content"). The scene titles in this file are only notes
  for us; do not paste them.

NEGATIVE (paste into the negative-prompt field, or end the prompt with "Avoid: ..."):
  Avoid: text, letters, logos, watermark, signature, frame, border, photograph, 3D render,
  plastic look, cartoon, anime, extra fingers, distorted hands, blurry, low resolution,
  cropped head, multiple people (unless asked), modern clothing (unless asked).

CUT-OUT LAYERS (L1, L2, L3), add this:
  Isolated on a transparent background (PNG with alpha). If the tool cannot do transparency:
  "isolated on a flat pure #00FF00 green background, no shadow on the background", then
  remove the green afterwards (remove.bg, Photoshop "Remove background", or rembg).

Tools that work well:
  - GPT-image (ChatGPT / OpenAI API): supports "transparent background" directly.
  - Midjourney: best paint quality; no transparency, so use the green background trick.
    Use --ar for aspect ratio, --style raw, and --sref <your L0 image URL> on every layer
    of a scene so all layers share one look.
  - Recraft / Ideogram: also export transparent PNGs.


3. YOUR FACE IN THE PAINTINGS (optional but recommended)
--------------------------------------------------------
For the hero figure to be you: give the tool 2-3 clear photos of your face as a character
reference (Midjourney: --cref <photo URL> --cw 60; GPT-image: attach the photo and say
"the man's face is based on the attached photo, painted in the style described"). Use the same
reference for every scene where "the scholar" appears, so he looks like one person.

5. HOW THE LAYERS WILL MOVE (so you know what each one is for)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Mouse:  each layer sits at its own depth (L0 far, L1 middle, L2 near, L3 nearest), so the
          scene turns in 3D: near layers shift most. Each layer also gets its own depth map
          (tools/make-depth.mjs) so the figure itself has relief, not just the stack.
  Scroll (detaching, like the reference):
          - L3 objects lift off and drift outward and toward the camera, slowly rotating
            (the laptop tilts open, the mug floats up, charts slide apart);
          - L1 figure leans or slides slightly toward the centre;
          - L2 foreground drops away at the bottom;
          - L0 background zooms in slowly;
          - then the scene turns into the silver line drawing with glitter and hands over to
            the next scene, as now.
