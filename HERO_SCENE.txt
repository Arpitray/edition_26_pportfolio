HERO SCENE - 2 THINGS TO MAKE
=============================

Start from the picture you already made: red velvet curtain at the top, blue sky with clouds
on the right, monitor with code and the hourglass on the desk at the left, the man from behind
drawing on his board. Call it the KEYFRAME.

The hero is built from 2 pieces:
  1. the room (everything except the man and his board), a still image
  2. the man with his board, a video (his hand draws), on blue


1. THE ROOM  (ChatGPT, image edit)
----------------------------------
Upload the KEYFRAME and paste:

Edit this painting: remove the man and his drawing board completely. Paint in what was behind
them: the rest of the dark carved wooden desk, the red velvet curtain and the blue sky with
white clouds, so it looks like the same room with nobody in it. Keep the monitor, the
hourglass, the curtain, the sky and the desk exactly as they are: same style, same colours,
same size, same position. Do not add anything new. Keep the 16:9 size.

Save as: L0 Background Plate.png


2. THE MAN, MOVING  (video tool: Kling, Luma or Runway)
-------------------------------------------------------
a) First the still, in ChatGPT. Upload the KEYFRAME and paste:

Edit this painting: keep only the man and his drawing board, exactly as they are, same size and
same position in the frame. Replace everything else (curtain, sky, desk, monitor, hourglass)
with a flat pure #0000FF blue. No shadow on the blue. His hair edges crisp and sharp against the blue, no blue light on him. Keep the 16:9 size.

Save as: L1 Figure - Builder.png

b) Then the video. In the video tool choose "image to video". Put L1 Figure - Builder.png as
the START frame and the same picture as the END frame (this makes the loop seamless). Length
5 to 8 seconds, 1080p. Paste:

Animate this exact painting. It stays an oil painting in every frame: same painted
brushstrokes, same colours, same painted face, hair and clothes; not realistic, not a photo,
not a film. Camera completely still. Only his right hand and forearm move, drawing slow small
pencil strokes on the paper, pausing, then drawing again. His shoulders rise and fall slightly
with breathing. Nothing else moves; the blue stays flat. Hair edges stay crisp. Seamless loop.

Negative prompt (if there is a field):
realistic, photorealistic, live action, new person, different face, camera movement, zoom,
morphing, flicker, extra fingers, background change, text

Export MP4. Save as: L1 Figure - Builder.mp4
